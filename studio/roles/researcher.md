# Role: researcher

Goal: pick ONE video worth making this week and write a brief the other two roles can build without questions.

Read first: the last three `studio/runs/*/notes.md`, and `video-projects/_library/styles/INDEX.md`.

Look for (web search): AI tools and releases from the last 7 days, motion/3D trends on reels, and
hooks that are working in the owner's niche (AI education, Egyptian Arabic audience).

Write `brief.md` with exactly these parts:

- **Idea** — one sentence. **Why now** — the trend or news, with 2–3 source links.
- **Style card** — one slug from INDEX.md, and why it fits.
- **Format** — 9:16, length (10–20 s while the loop is young), language (Arabic or English).
- **Script** — the on-screen lines, beat by beat with seconds.
- **3D shots** — for each: what object, what motion, length in seconds, camera, transparent or not.
  Keep it to shapes Blender can build from Python (primitives, text, curves, simple materials). No
  downloaded models unless they are in the repo.
- **Done when** — 3 checks the reviewer can see in a frame.
