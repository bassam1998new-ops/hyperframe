# AurorA Studio — Mood V2

Mood is an executable creative contract, not a list of adjectives.

## Core rule
Keep mood tool-agnostic. Describe the result before choosing HyperFrames, Blender or After Effects.

Bad:
- cinematic
- premium
- futuristic

Better:
- first 20% feels quiet and curious
- middle builds controlled tension with tighter framing and faster motion
- payoff opens into deeper space and brighter dimensional light
- final 10% becomes still and confident

## Create mood.json
For a production run, create the mood artifact and fill:
- one-sentence intent
- what the audience should feel
- product truth that cannot be lost
- emotional/energy/tension arc
- composition and depth
- camera behavior and lens feel
- lighting
- palette roles
- typography
- materials / texture
- subject/camera/graphic motion
- transition grammar
- edit rhythm
- voice/music/SFX/silence
- continuity anchors
- what must not happen
- assumptions + confidence

## Arc
Use normalized ranges 0–1 so the mood survives duration changes.
Energy and tension use 0–10.

## Continuity anchors
Choose a few things that make the whole video feel like one piece, for example:
- one camera behavior
- one material family
- one recurring shape
- one lighting direction
- one sound motif

## Must-not-happen
Be specific. Examples:
- no generic floating neon particles
- no random camera moves
- no glossy 3D if the product is calm/editorial
- no stock-looking AI people
- no text inside generated images when HyperFrames can typeset it cleanly

## Tool routing
Do not choose the implementation tool inside Mood.
After Mood is clear, requirements + asset search + routing decide the production path.
